Rent Portal records rent bills and payments between landlords and tenants. Rent is paid directly to the landlord through M-Pesa Paybill; the portal does not hold or transfer money.

Accounts

Landlord accounts are admitted by the site owner. Tenant accounts are approved by the landlord of the plot. You must give accurate information and must not submit payment messages that are altered or belong to someone else. Landlords may reject such payments and remove the tenant's account.

Payments

A payment appears as confirmed only after the landlord checks it against their M-Pesa statement and approves it. Disputes about rent are between the landlord and tenant.
