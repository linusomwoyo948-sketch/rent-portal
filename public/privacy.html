Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Rent Portal (https://rent-portal-kenyaapp.netlify.app) helps landlords in Kenya collect and record rent, and helps tenants submit and track their rent payments. This page explains what information the portal keeps and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google or with an email link, the portal receives your email address and your name. Tenants also provide their phone number, plot name and house or unit number. When a tenant submits a payment, the portal stores the M-Pesa confirmation message they paste, including the transaction code, amount, date and the account it was paid to. Landlords provide their name, phone number, location and plot details, including Paybill and account numbers.

How the information is used

The information is used only to run the portal. Landlords and their caretakers see the details of tenants in their own plots so they can approve tenants, confirm payments, issue receipts and prepare statements and reports. Tenants see only their own account, bills and payments. The site owner sees landlord accounts and applications in order to admit and manage landlords.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers. Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and the site is hosted by Netlify. These providers process the data only to operate the service.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, the portal requests only your name and email address. It does not access your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Keeping and deleting information

Payment records are kept for as long as the landlord's account is active so that rent history and receipts remain available. A tenant can ask their landlord to remove their account, and a landlord can ask the site owner to remove theirs. To request deletion or a copy of your information, email linusomwoyo96@gmail.com.

Security

Access to each account requires signing in, and each person can see only the information their role allows. Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
